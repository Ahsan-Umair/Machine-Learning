------------------------------------------------------------
1) WHAT THIS PROJECT IS
------------------------------------------------------------

This project tries to answer one question:

"If a person was on the Titanic, would they live or die?"

The computer looks at clues like:
- their ticket class
- their gender
- their age
- how much they paid
- whether they were alone
- whether they had a cabin
- their title like Mr, Mrs, Miss, Dr

Real-world example:
This is like guessing whether a student will finish homework by looking at things like:
- how much time they have
- whether they are alone
- whether they are busy
- whether they have help

11) FIXING MISSING EMBARKED VALUES
------------------------------------------------------------

Code:
df['Embarked'] = df['Embarked'].fillna(df['Embarked'].mode()[0])

Meaning:
Missing Embarked values are filled with the most common value.

Embarked means:
Which port the person boarded from.

Real-world example:
If most students choose one lunch option and a few forget to answer, you can use the most common choice.

Notebook note:
mode() = most common value.

------------------------------------------------------------
12) FIXING MISSING AGE VALUES
------------------------------------------------------------

Code:
df['Age'] = df.groupby(['Pclass', 'Sex'])['Age'].transform(
    lambda x: x.fillna(x.median())
)

Meaning:
Missing ages are filled using the median age of similar passengers.

Why:
People in the same class and sex may have similar age patterns.

Real-world example:
If a student's age is missing, you may guess it based on students in the same grade and group.

Notebook note:
median = middle value, not average.
